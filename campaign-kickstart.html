01The idea

Seasons create the deadlines.

Successful fundraising depends on a healthy pipeline, and seasons create the deadlines. The Campaign Calendar Kickstart gives your team a clear plan for the season ahead: what to say, to whom, and when.

02What you get
  • A 90-minute planning session with your team
  • A one-page campaign calendar covering 8 to 10 weeks
  • A messaging outline for each stage: welcome, appeal, reminder, and thank-you
  • Suggested subject lines and headlines for the first two sends
  • A short recap with priorities
03When it fits

Year-end

Advent begins November 29 and Giving Tuesday is December 1. To be ready, plan by mid-October.

Lent and Easter

Plan in January and February for Lent, Easter, and spring appeals.

It also works any time for a capital campaign, a gala, or an anniversary.
