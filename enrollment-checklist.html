Free Checklist for Enrollment & Admissions Teams

The Seventeen-Year-Old Test

Ten questions to see whether your admissions messaging works for the student reading it, and the parent reading over her shoulder.

Most institutions don’t have a storytelling problem. They have a capacity problem. This checklist helps you see where your viewbook, web pages, and email sequences are working and where they aren’t, in about ten minutes and without hiring anyone.

How to use it. Look at your most-used viewbook page, your top program page, and your first admitted-student email. For each question, mark Yes (2 points), Partly (1), or Not yet (0).
